# Writing Standards

Public-facing portfolio writing should prioritize evidence over adjectives.

## Standards

- Avoid inflated claims.
- Clearly distinguish individual work from team work.
- Explain technical decisions rather than merely naming technologies.
- Prefer concrete examples over generic skill assertions.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Use technical terminology accurately.
- Acknowledge tradeoffs.
- Contextualize mistakes or failed approaches when they demonstrate growth.
- Never imply mastery that the evidence does not support.
- Provide enough technical depth to invite intelligent questions.

## Claim Quality

Avoid:

> I developed a robust and scalable inventory architecture.

Prefer evidence-shaped wording:

> I replaced direct inventory/UI dependencies with an event-driven update path so item state could change without each inventory component needing references to every UI consumer.

The exact wording must depend on the evidence in [[Evidence Ledger]].

## Voice

- Use first person when describing personal decisions and implementation.
- Use team language when describing shared work.
- Do not hide uncertainty. Convert it into follow-up work.
- Do not make weak projects sound impressive through prose.
